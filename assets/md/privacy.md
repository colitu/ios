# Colitu Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 11, 2026**

This summary describes how the Colitu Secure VPN app and the Colitu service handle
your information. The authoritative version is published at
[colitu.com/legal/privacy](https://colitu.com/legal/privacy).

---

## Account data

To operate and protect the service, Colitu processes your account identity
(email address), registered device records (a random device key, device name,
platform, app and OS version), session metadata and aggregate traffic counters
used for plan limits. Colitu does not request IMEI or MAC identifiers.

## Payments

Payment details are entered on the hosted Platega checkout page. Colitu stores
order and provider transaction status only; it never stores card numbers, CVV,
bank details or wallet secrets.

## VPN configuration

VPN profiles are issued by the Colitu control plane for your device and are kept
in the device's protected storage (iOS Keychain / App Group file protection).

## Security and retention

Access is limited by purpose. Retention and deletion follow legal and
operational requirements.

## Contact

For privacy requests, email [support@colitu.com](mailto:support@colitu.com).
